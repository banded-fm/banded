Banded

Banded is a graph-powered music discovery tool that maps the relationships between musical artists. Instead of recommending music from listening history, it shows the real-world connections between artists, like shared band members, side projects, collaborations and tour support, that make up music scenes.

What you can do

  • Graph: search artists and walk through their network: band members, side projects and collaborators.
  • Map: explore the world's music scenes, found from clusters of connected artists.
  • Proximity: find out how close any artist is to a list of artists you already like, and see how they connect.
  • Upcoming: find upcoming concerts in a city, ranked by how well they fit the artists you like.
  • Play: Six Degrees of Music, a daily puzzle. Connect two artists through the people and bands between them, or make your own puzzle and share it.
  • About: how it works and FAQs.

Where the data comes from

Artist relationships come from MusicBrainz (band members, recording credits, producers), concert history from setlist.fm, and upcoming concerts from Pollstar. Connections and shows may be missing because the data is incomplete.

Banded is free and open source: github.com/banded-fm/banded. Banded runs in your browser and needs JavaScript.
